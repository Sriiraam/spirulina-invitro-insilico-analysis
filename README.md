## ⏣ Project Highlights
◆ **Spirulina Bioactivity Profiling**  
• Conducted DPPH, MTT (on 3T3 cells), and Fe³⁺ reducing assays to assess antioxidant and cytotoxic activity.  
• Performed antimicrobial testing via zone of inhibition method.  
• Identified major compounds using GC-MS, FTIR, and UV-Vis spectroscopy.  
• Modeled molecular interactions using advanced bioinformatics tools.

---

## 🧪 Wet Lab Workflow (Experimental Phase)

### 🔬 Sample Preparation
- Spirulina biomass extracted using ethanol/methanol solvents.

### 🧫 Bioactivity Assays
- **DPPH Assay**: Measured radical scavenging capacity.
- **Fe³⁺ Reducing Assay**: Evaluated electron-donating potential.
- **MTT Assay (3T3 cells)**: Assessed cytotoxicity and cell viability.
- **Zone of Inhibition**: Tested antimicrobial activity against selected pathogens.

### 🧬 Compound Identification
- **GC-MS**: Profiled volatile bioactive compounds.
- **FTIR**: Identified functional groups and molecular fingerprints.
- **UV-Vis Spectroscopy**: Analyzed absorbance peaks of active compounds.

---

## 💻 Dry Lab Workflow (Computational Phase)

### 🧠 Step-by-Step Bioinformatics Pipeline

| Stage               | Tool/Platform           | Purpose                                      |
|--------------------|-------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Protein Retrieval   | **PDB**                 | Downloaded target bacterial protein structures |
| Ligand Design       | **ChemSketch**          | Sketched GC-MS identified compounds           |
| Ligand Optimization | **ArgusLab**            | Energy minimization and geometry refinement   |
| Docking Prep        | **PDB + ArgusLab**      | Prepared protein and ligand files for docking |
| Visualization       | **RasMol**              | Visualized molecular interactions in 3D       |
| High-Res Imaging    | **PyMOL Viewer**        | Generated cinematic snapshots of docking poses |

---

## 📈 Key Findings
- Spirulina compounds demonstrated potent antioxidant and antimicrobial activity.
- MTT assay confirmed low cytotoxicity on 3T3 cells.
- FTIR and UV-Vis validated compound identity and purity.
- Docking revealed strong binding affinity with bacterial targets.
